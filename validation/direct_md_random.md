# Random-vacancy survival, Mishin Cu

The vacancy is the lattice site nearest the cell center. The <100> dumbbell host is drawn uniformly from the other sites of the 13³ cell, and the dumbbell axis uniformly from the three orientations. Under periodic boundaries the separation is uniform over the cell, which is the ensemble in section 2 of Mwungeli, Liu, and Demkowicz, Phys. Rev. Materials 9, 023601 (2025). The fit is ln(n(t)/N) = -(k/V_model) t through the origin, sampled every 25 ps. V_iv/Ω = 4k / (α a D_i) with α = 2/3, Ω = a³/4, and D_i from Table 1 of that paper (quoted from Ref. [12]). The quoted error is the standard deviation over 400 bootstrap resamples of the trajectories and does not include the Table 1 uncertainty on D_i.

At the longest window, 1500 ps, V_iv = 289.44 ± 25.29 Ω.
Table 1 gives D_i = 0.526 ± 0.006 Å²/ps. That relative uncertainty scales this volume by 3.30 Ω.
Table 1 at this temperature, 8788 atoms, is 185 ± 23 Ω (k-method) and 161 ± 15 Ω (mean-time method). The longest-window volume minus the k-method value is 104.44 Ω.
