=StrainResponse= is one hop, for any potential and cell. =ElasticDipole::from_stress= is =P = -V (σ_defect - σ_bulk)=, the sign of Varvenne and Clouet, Phys. Rev. B 96, 224103 (2017), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.96.224103. =barrier= returns the lowest point-group image of =ΔE0 - (P_saddle - P_minimum):ε=, and =NeedsSearch= when a radius, a quadratic error bound, or the softening of the unstable mode is past its limit, or when the barrier falls through the floor. =refine= runs a fixed-cell solid dimer from the strained saddle and caches the index-1 result. =to_json= is the event record. Dederichs and Schroeder, Phys. Rev. B 17, 2524 (1978), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.17.2524, show that a homogeneous strain changes the jump rate through the saddle dipole. On the Mishin copper dumbbell the quadratic bound fitted at 0.5 percent shear stays inside 0.01 eV, and at 2 percent it asks for a search. The =[101]= image of that hop has the same linear barrier under =ε_zx= as the =[110]= image under =ε_yx=.
