The copper rate catalogue hop searches and the 8788-atom capture campaigns no longer read a configuration from wherever a step budget left it. Their placements, reactants, products and pushes off a saddle follow the ODE12r flow to 1e-3 eV/Å (2e-4 eV/Å for PAFI end states), and a saddle reaches recombination when its push relaxes into a basin with every lattice site singly occupied. The capped test helpers (~relax_fast~, ~relax_pair_axis~, ~quench_bound~, ~pair_recombined~, ~occupied~, ~basin_is_dumbbell~ and ~reaches_recombination~) are gone, and an unconverged relaxation stops a campaign.
The spontaneous sites of the run-13 volume came from a census of one representative vector per radius, relaxed by 60 FIRE steps, some then quenched by up to 200 more, and judged by a gap window, which gave 34 sites at the Table 1 lattices. =benchmarks/defect_pair_census.csv= is the flow census of all 132 axis orbits within 10.8 Å in the 13³ cell on those lattices, each checked by steepest descent under a 0.02 Å cap and converged within 393 ODE12r steps; 46 sites recombine at 300 K and 42 at 500 and 700 K, including the (3 3 0) orbit at 7.70 Å, which shares its radius with (4 1 1). It took 23.6 min on 8 threads. A lumped state of one radius and dumbbell axis is now spontaneous in the fraction of its sites whose orbit recombines.
The isolated-dumbbell translation and rotation PAFI rows were rerun from converged end states on the lattices of the recorded rows, into =benchmarks/defect_pair_isolated.csv= (21.9 min on 8 threads for all six). The zero-temperature barriers move by -1.1, -1.1 and -0.07 meV for translation and -6.0, -8.2 and +0.2 meV for rotation at 300, 500 and 700 K. ΔF moves from 0.0925±0.0055, 0.0940±0.0092 and 0.0729±0.0091 eV to 0.0818±0.0054, 0.0776±0.0078 and 0.0672±0.0103 eV for translation, and from 0.2014±0.0087, 0.1644±0.0166 and 0.1140±0.0154 eV to 0.1661±0.0101, 0.1384±0.0142 and 0.1142±0.0153 eV for rotation. The recorded 300 and 500 K rows came from an earlier campaign with a different band and attempt frequency (2.1–2.3×10¹² Hz against 1.4×10¹² Hz here and in the recorded 700 K rows), so those changes are not convergence alone. κ is not resolved in any of the six (errors 0.18–0.58), so at 700 K the translation rate is still the direct-MD channel rate; at 300 and 500 K the MD run has too few jumps and the PAFI rate is used.
=benchmarks/defect_pair_converged_volume.csv= is the run-13 rate model on these inputs, with one-sigma errors from 64 draws of the translation, rotation and capture rates within their errors. At the 30 Å cutoff the 13³-cell decay volume is 202.7±18.8, 216.1±33.6 and 179.2±16.5 Ω at 300, 500 and 700 K (was 156.1, 145.8 and 136.9 Ω), the mean-time volume 209.9±20.4, 224.5±37.0 and 184.5±17.9 Ω (was 159.8, 148.9 and 139.6 Ω), and the free sphere 131.9±8.3, 137.7±14.0 and 121.3±7.3 Ω (was 109.9, 104.6 and 99.9 Ω). Table 1 at 8788 atoms is 254±21 / 286±28, 185±23 / 161±15 and 203±10 / 210±21 Ω. The census alone, with the recorded rates (=benchmarks/defect_pair_census_volume.csv=), gives 190.3, 183.6 and 179.3 Ω for the decay volume. The assembly took 7.1 min with the draws.
The capture, rotation, away and switch barriers of the shells near the vacancy (=defect_pair_capture.csv=, =defect_pair_local.csv=, =defect_pair_run9.csv=, =defect_pair_tight.csv=, =defect_pair_run11.csv=, =defect_pair_viv.csv= and the rotation and switch rows of =defect_pair_run12.csv= and =defect_pair_run13.csv=) were not rerun; the converged volumes reuse them. The direct-MD volumes, which start from the ideal dumbbell, do not depend on these relaxations. The run-13 volumes from the recorded inputs are unchanged.
