~SaddleIndexCheck~ certifies the saddle index of a converged band's climbing image, min-mode search or OCI-NEB climbing image: block Lanczos on forward-difference Hessian actions, seeded with the search's mode and pseudo-random vectors, estimates the lowest curvatures on the free coordinates and counts those under ~-negative_tol~.
Fixed atoms, the rigid motions a band removes and the coordinates a host zeroes in the gradient are left out.
~BandConfig::saddle_check~ and ~MinModeConfig::saddle_check~ hold it, ~BandReport::saddle_index~, ~MinModeReport::saddle_index~ and ~OcinebReport::saddle_index~ carry the ~SaddleIndex~ of the converged step, and ~saddle_evaluations~ sums the check's evaluations over a session.
~rgsaddle::saddle_index~ certifies one point of a ~PointSurface~.
~SaddleIndexCheck::escape~, off by default, takes a ~SaddleEscape~: at index two or more the point is pushed along each negative mode but the reaction mode, to that mode's lower-energy side, and the search climbs again, the downward search of Yin, Wang, Chen, Zhang, and Zhang, Phys. Rev. Lett. 124, 090601 (2020).
On the 37-atom Morse slab of ~tests/band_fixed_atoms.rs~ every stepper stops its climbing image on the mirror at -50.6503755, and the check reports index 3 there in 58 to 60 evaluations at ~force_tol~ 1e-3, with the curvatures -1.3798, -0.1640 and -0.0590 for FIRE.
With the escape, FIRE at ~force_tol~ 1e-4 reaches the first-order saddle at -50.6761677129, index 1, curvature -0.9133, 0.559 Å off the mirror, in 264 force rows, and Lanczos started on the mirror point reaches it in 1292 evaluations.
The C ABI moves to minor 40 with ~rgsaddle_saddle_check_t~, ~rgsaddle_saddle_index_t~, ~rgsaddle_saddle_check_default~, ~rgsaddle_band_set_saddle_check~, ~rgsaddle_minmode_set_saddle_check~, ~rgsaddle_ocineb_set_saddle_check~, the matching ~_saddle_index~ calls and ~rgsaddle_saddle_index~.
The C++ ~Band~, ~MinMode~ and ~Ocineb~ gain ~set_saddle_check~ and ~saddle_index~, and Python adds ~SessionReport.saddle_index~, ~MinMode.saddle_index~, ~MinMode.set_saddle_check~, ~rgsaddle.saddle_index~, ~rgsaddle.ase.saddle_index~ and ~rgsaddle.ase.NEB.saddle_index~.
