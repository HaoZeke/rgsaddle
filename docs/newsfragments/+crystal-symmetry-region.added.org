=SymmetryUniqueRegion::from_crystal_operations= lifts generic
three-dimensional crystal operations, explicit or species-matched atom
permutations, and reactant/saddle geometries into a hard region on one
hop's dividing plane.  Periodic matching uses =minimage=.  The path
multiplicity is the number of distinct saddle images under the reactant
stabilizer and is the argument of =RateMultiplicity::with_paths=.  The
region degeneracy is the order of the operations that stabilize both
endpoints, which is the in-plane symmetry of that one hop, and the sampled
cell is the asymmetric unit of the plane.  Jóhannesson and Jónsson, /The
Journal of Chemical Physics/ 115, 9644-9656 (2001),
doi:10.1063/1.1415499, Secs. II D and III D, sample one symmetric part of a
hyperplane this way.  The twelve fcc nearest-neighbour vacancy directions
are a path multiplicity, not that region degeneracy.
