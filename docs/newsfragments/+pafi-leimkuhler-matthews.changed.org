~average_hyperplane~ steps its overdamped walk as Leimkuhler and Matthews, Appl. Math. Res. Express 2013, 34 (2013), doi:10.1093/amrx/abs010, Eq. (3), the high-friction limit of their BAOAB step: the noise of a step is the sum of a new Gaussian draw and the draw of the step before, and a step is still one force call.
Stationary averages are second order in ~PafiConfig::time_step~ instead of first, and a harmonic plane is sampled exactly at any stable step; at ~μ k dt = 1.2~ the Euler–Maruyama step it replaces samples ~⟨y²⟩~ 2.5 times too wide.
On six planes of the W vacancy hop of Swinburne and Marinica's reference code (github.com/tomswinburne/pafi, commit 1661a1d) at 1000 K, 400 000 steps each, the Euler–Maruyama gradients at ~μ dt = 8 × 10⁻³~ were 2.8σ to 9.4σ, up to 0.083 eV per unit ~r~, from those of the new step at the same ~μ dt~, which agree with the new step at ~10⁻³~ to χ² per plane 0.86 and have five to seven times its effective samples.
At ~10⁻³~ the Euler–Maruyama gradients of that hop differed from the new ones by at most 0.010 eV per unit ~r~ at 1000 K and 0.027 at 2000 K, and its barriers by at most 0.00025 eV and 0.0038 eV.
The same ~PafiConfig::seed~ gives other trajectories than before.
