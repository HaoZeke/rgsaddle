=LinebConfig::with_gradient_end_tangents(true)= ends the orbit spline of a fixed-energy LI-NEB path along the surface gradient at each turning point. The not-a-knot ends remain the default. On a curved band the turning replica of the reconstructed ring otherwise keeps a force residual of |∇V| sin θ, with θ the angle between the spline end tangent and the gradient.
