=ArtSession= runs ART nouveau as eight persisted phases: a host seed and displacement mask, perpendicular relaxation, Lanczos, a lowest-mode climb, an index-1 certificate, a push both ways along the unstable mode, an unconstrained quench of the two images, and a reversibility report. =run_events= marks duplicate saddles. =ArtStatus::Reverted= is a quench pair that falls in one basin and is not a completed exit. Every surface evaluation is listed on =ArtSession::force_calls=. Malek and Mousseau, Phys. Rev. E 62, 7723 (2000), doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.62.7723.
