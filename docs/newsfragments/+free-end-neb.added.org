~BandConfig::with_free_end~, ~BandSession::set_free_end~, ~FreeEndConfig~, ~FreeEndSide~, ~FreeEndReport~ (~FeaNebReport~), and ~FeaNebSession~ add the free-end adaptive nudged elastic band (FEA-NEB) method of Jiayong Zhang, Hongwu Zhang, Hongfei Ye, and Yonggang Zheng, J. Chem. Phys. 145, 094104 (2016), doi:10.1063/1.4962019, and the level-set free-end formulation of Ting Zhu, Ju Li, Amit Samanta, Hyoung Gyu Kim, and Subra Suresh, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 104, 3031 (2007), doi:10.1073/pnas.0611097104.
One terminal image moves subject to \(E(x_{\mathrm{end}}) = E_{\mathrm{target}}\). Its force is Zhang et al. Eq. (7), the potential force perpendicular to the path, with no spring. Net translation is removed before that force is projected onto the level set, and ~rgmin::Solver~ with ~LevelSetConstraint~ retracts the endpoint onto the target energy. The endpoint gradient must be nonzero. A refused endpoint curvature pair is a stalled step and does not move the endpoint. The step reports ~target_energy_residual~ and ~BandSession::evaluation~ at the positions it accepted. Adaptive cuts keep a local window around the peak and are counted only when images are removed. The remesh spaces images evenly along that window and, when the free terminal image lies outside it, along the leg that reaches the free end, so the free end stays an endpoint. ~BandSession::step_free_end~ and ~FeaNebSession::step~ run a latched minimum-mode refinement when climbing is on, the same way ~BandSession::step~ does. Fixed endpoints stay the default.
C ABI minor 45 adds ~rgsaddle_band_set_free_end~, ~rgsaddle_band_free_end_residual~, and ~rgsaddle_band_free_end_cuts~ (~Band::set_free_end~, ~Band::free_end_residual~, and ~Band::free_end_cuts~ in C++, and ~Band.set_free_end~, ~Band.target_energy_residual~, and ~Band.free_end_cuts~ in Python). A host checks ~rgsaddle_abi_version()~ for minor 45 or newer. The simplified string and the growing string stay at minor 44.
