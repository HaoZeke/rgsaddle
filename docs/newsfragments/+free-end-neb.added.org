~BandConfig::with_free_end~, ~BandSession::set_free_end~, ~FreeEndConfig~, ~FreeEndSide~, ~FreeEndReport~ (~FeaNebReport~), and ~FeaNebSession~ add the free-end adaptive nudged elastic band (FEA-NEB) method of Jiayong Zhang, Hongwu Zhang, Hongfei Ye, and Yonggang Zheng, J. Chem. Phys. 145, 094104 (2016), doi:10.1063/1.4962019, and the level-set free-end formulation of Ting Zhu, Ju Li, Amit Samanta, Hyoung Gyu Kim, and Subra Suresh, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 104, 3031 (2007), doi:10.1073/pnas.0611097104.
One terminal image moves subject to \(E(x_{\mathrm{end}}) = E_{\mathrm{target}}\). Its force is Zhang et al. Eq. (7), the potential force perpendicular to the path, with no spring. Net translation is removed before that force is projected onto the level set, and ~rgmin::Solver~ with ~LevelSetConstraint~ retracts the endpoint onto the target energy. The endpoint gradient must be nonzero. The step reports ~target_energy_residual~ and ~BandSession::evaluation~ at the positions it accepted. Adaptive cuts keep a local window around the peak and are counted only when images are removed. Fixed endpoints stay the default.
C ABI minor 45 adds ~rgsaddle_band_set_free_end~, ~rgsaddle_band_free_end_residual~, and ~rgsaddle_band_free_end_cuts~ (~Band::set_free_end~, ~Band::free_end_residual~, and ~Band::free_end_cuts~ in C++, and ~Band.set_free_end~, ~Band.target_energy_residual~, and ~Band.free_end_cuts~ in Python). A host checks ~rgsaddle_abi_version()~ for minor 45 or newer. The simplified string and the growing string stay at minor 44.
