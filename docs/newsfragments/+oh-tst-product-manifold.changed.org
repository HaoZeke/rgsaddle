~PlaneOptimizer~ climbs the plane free energy by rgmin's Riemannian trust region (~rtr::minimize_rtr_gauged~) on the product of the unit sphere of normals, off the sampler's held modes or within ~PlaneOptimizerConfig::rotation_basis~, and a Euclidean factor for the offset along the normal; the gauge hook moves the pivot to the centroid of each accepted plane's sample.
~PlaneMetric::Blocks~ weights the sphere by the square of ~max_translation~ over ~max_rotation~, and ~PlaneMetric::Harmonic~ makes the harmonic Hessian at a reference point, from Lanczos steps of Hessian actions, the sphere's ~TangentMetric~.
Every plane is sampled from the start plane's point with one seed, and the trust region tests the change of the free energy by the trapezoid of the sampled gradients between the current plane and the trial.
~PlaneOptimizer::new~ takes the start plane and the settings and ~run~ the surface; the manifold parameter, ~step~ and ~PlaneStep~ are gone, and the test fixture ~PointSphereFixture~ with them.
~PlaneOptimizerConfig~ drops ~time_step~, ~adapt_time_step~ and ~warm_burn_in~ and gains ~metric~, ~metric_rank~ and ~rotation_basis~; ~PlaneOptimum~ adds the inner iterations, the accepted planes and the planes sampled, and its trace holds the accepted planes.
From the LEPS dimer plane at ~kT = 0.1 E_A~ the climb takes 6 trust-region iterations and 389069 force calls, against 20 quick-min steps and 807875 force calls before; in 48 dimensions, from the saddle plane turned 0.3 rad, 13 iterations and 475146 force calls, against 36 steps and 1210226 force calls.
