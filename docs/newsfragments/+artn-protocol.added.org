=ArtNConfig= adds the opt-in ART nouveau basin-exit and activation protocol to =MinModeSession= and the point builder. It delays Lanczos during random-direction pushes, relaxes perpendicular to each push, follows the negative mode during activation, and warm-starts each Lanczos solve from the previous direction. Ordinary dimer and Lanczos configurations remain unchanged. C ABI minor 43 adds =rgsaddle_artn_config_t=, its default initializer and the live-session setter. Malek and Mousseau, Phys. Rev. E 62, 7723 (2000), doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.62.7723; Marinica, Willaime, and Mousseau, Phys. Rev. B 83, 094119 (2011), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.83.094119; Jay et al., J. Chem. Theory Comput. 16, 6726 (2020), doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.0c00541.
