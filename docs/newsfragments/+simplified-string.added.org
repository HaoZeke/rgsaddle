~BandConfig::with_string(StringConfig::default())~ and ~BandSession::set_string~ add the simplified string method of E, Ren, and Vanden-Eijnden, J. Chem. Phys. 126, 164103 (2007), doi:10.1063/1.2720838, as an opt-in band method beside NEB.
Interior images evolve under the full potential force (no spring, no tangent projection) through the band's ~rgmin::Solver~ steppers (~FIRE~, ~L-BFGS~, ~QuickMin~, ~Bb~, and ~ODE12r~) and are placed at equal arc length after each step on the not-a-knot cubic spline (~StringConfig::spline()~, default) or on the polyline (~StringConfig::linear()~).
When ~BandConfig::climbing~ arms an interior image, that image reflects the potential force along the path tangent and stays anchored during reparametrization while each side is spaced at equal arc length on its own, with ~MinModeRefine~, ~SaddleIndexCheck~, ~ExpBandPrecon~, ~Cell~, ~fixed_atoms~, and ~set_image_threads~ supported as on an atomic band.
On the 25-image Müller-Brown path between minima A and B (=tests/simplified_string.rs=), the spline string reaches a maximum deviation of 0.0111 from the steepest-descent minimum energy path (against 0.0144 for the polyline string and 0.0305 for 25-image CI-NEB), and the climbing string locates both barriers' saddles within 0.001 of the analytic saddles and CI-NEB.
On the 107-atom Morse Cu fcc vacancy hop (=tests/makri_vacancy.rs=, 5 images, ~L-BFGS~ memory 20, tolerance 0.001 eV/Å), a rerun of that test measured the spline string at 37 steps (75.67 evaluations per interior image, 1.84369893 eV), the climbing spline string at 35 steps (71.67 evaluations per image, 1.84369903 eV), and the linear string at 149 steps (1.84370066 eV), against NEB at 18 steps (19.67 evaluations per image, 1.84369917 eV).
C ABI minor 44 adds ~RGSADDLE_BAND_STRING~ and ~rgsaddle_band_set_string~ (~Band::set_string~ in C++), and Python adds ~rgsaddle.Band(..., string=True)~, ~_core.project_band(..., projection="string")~, and ~rgsaddle.ase.SimplifiedString~.
