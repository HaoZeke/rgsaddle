The C band config carries =refine_method= and =refine_threshold= from ABI minor 45, mirroring the =opt_method= and =threshold= of eOn's =[Refine]= section with eOn's defaults: =RGSADDLE_REFINE_NONE=, which steps with the band method alone, and a threshold of 0.5, where zero is the default. =RGSADDLE_REFINE_LBFGS= steps every band step whose convergence force is at or under the threshold with L-BFGS of the config's memory, and any other step with the band method, so a force that rises back over the threshold returns the band to FIRE. A config older than minor 45 never reads the two members, so =RGSADDLE_METHOD_FIRE= is FIRE alone at every minor. A negative or NaN threshold and an unknown refine code are refused at create.
