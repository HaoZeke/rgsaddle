A periodic atomic band now keeps each neighbour-link image shift
continuous between steps using =minimage='s Euclidean displacement
instead of rewrapping the link from scratch on every assembly.
The simplified string method uses the same stored shifts, including
after a step replaces the band. Slabs and wires, including a skewed
slab, take their image translations from =linkcell= 0.4.2 and
=minimage= 0.2.3. A nonfinite or dependent cell is rejected by the
band, trust-region force assembly, growing string, and AutoNEB
constructors. Position replacement by zoom, RTR, or min-mode
refinement still refreshes the shifts. This avoids force sign flips
at half cell cuts and keeps host-wrapped handoffs and skewed links
on the same physical image.
