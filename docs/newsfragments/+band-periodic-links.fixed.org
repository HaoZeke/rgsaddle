A periodic band keeps the lattice translation of each link between neighbouring images from one step to the next.
It used to wrap every link to its minimum image again at each assembly.
Once an atom sat more than half a cell from its counterpart in the next image, the wrapped displacement jumped by a lattice vector, and the tangent and the spring force jumped with it.
A band that relaxes toward that cut sat on it: the force changed sign for a move of a few ulp, FIRE never saw a positive power, its time step halved to nothing, and the step ended in =SaddleError::Stalled= with the force still far above the gate.
The 7 image NEB and CI-NEB on the Pt(111) exchange system stopped that way at iteration 483.
They now keep stepping.
=BandSession::set_positions= recomputes the translation of a link beside a replaced row, and rows that differ from the session's own only by a lattice vector are still taken as unchanged.
Bands with every link under half a cell are bit for bit as before.
