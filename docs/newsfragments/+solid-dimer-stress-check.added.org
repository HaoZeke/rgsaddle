=StressCheck= and =check_cauchy_stress= compare =PointSurface::cauchy_stress= with =finite_difference_cauchy_stress= of the same surface's energy, and =SolidDimerConfig::with_stress_check= runs that comparison once before the first free-cell step. A disagreement is =SaddleError::Surface= naming the worst entry and the factor between the two tensors: -1 for a pressure tensor in place of the stress, 1.602e6 for a stress in bar, -1.602e6 for a LAMMPS =metal= pressure passed through, and no factor for permuted components. It costs eighteen energies once; a surface without =cauchy_stress= is not checked.
A copper vacancy hop with the diagonal strains held and the shears free kept a largest generalized force of 0.63–4.2 eV/Å after 40 and 160 steps while its cell sheared, and the frozen cell converged. On rgpot's Cu_u3 EAM through the same =PointSurface= path, with =cauchy_stress= returning σ, the three free masks converge in 41–49 steps from the frozen-cell saddle, and the analytic cell force matches finite differences of the enthalpy within 8e-8 eV/Å for all 63 free masks. Returning the pressure tensor =-σ= reproduces the report: with the diagonals held the largest force is 0.30, 6.3 and 4.2 eV/Å at steps 1, 40 and 160 and the tilt is still growing, because the cell block of the gradient is =(V/J) σ= and the flipped sign walks every free strain uphill. Weights of 0.25 and 4 do not converge it either. A stress in bar starts at 1.3e4 eV/Å and an unconverted LAMMPS pressure at 2.1e5 eV/Å instead.
=PointSurface::cauchy_stress= states the sign, the units and the LAMMPS conversion. =SolidDimerConfig::external_stress= is described as the pressure-signed load it is, not as a Cauchy stress.

Diagonals held with the shears free converge in 40 steps at an unchanged volume, shears held in 40, and the pressure-signed surface is refused before the first step.
