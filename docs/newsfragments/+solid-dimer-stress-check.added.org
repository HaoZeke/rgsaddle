=StressCheck= and =check_cauchy_stress= compare =PointSurface::cauchy_stress= with =finite_difference_cauchy_stress= of the same surface's energy entry by entry, each against 1e-6 plus 1% of its own magnitude, and =SolidDimerConfig::with_stress_check= runs that comparison once before the first free-cell step. A disagreement is =SaddleError::Surface= naming the worst entry and the factor between the two tensors: -1 for a pressure tensor in place of the stress, 1.602e6 for a stress in bar, -1.602e6 for a LAMMPS =metal= pressure passed through, and no factor for permuted components. When every diagonal entry agrees the message says so, which leaves the shear convention or a box that misses tilts. It costs eighteen energies once; a surface without =cauchy_stress= is not checked.
A copper vacancy hop with the diagonal strains held and the shears free kept a largest generalized force of 0.63–4.2 eV/Å after 40 and 160 steps while its cell sheared, though its stress matched finite differences on the diagonal, the frozen cell converged in 13 steps, and the fully free cell converged with a shear of about 0.1% of the edge. Held diagonals with free shears are the only mask that moves the cell at a fixed diagonal and volume, and their cell force reads only =σ_yx=, =σ_zx= and =σ_zy=, bit for bit independent of the diagonal. On rgpot's Cu_u3 EAM with the stress right, the three free masks converge in 41–49 steps from the frozen-cell saddle, and the analytic cell force matches finite differences of the enthalpy within 8e-8 eV/Å for all 63 masks. A box refreshed only when lx, ly or lz change reproduces the report in an untilted cell: frozen, fully free (43 steps, tilt 0.007 Å) and shears-held runs converge, while with the diagonals held the tilt reaches -0.48 Å in 160 steps and the force in the true cell 1.2 eV/Å. Its diagonal matches finite differences to 2e-10 eV/Å³, and its shear gap of 2.2e-4 eV/Å³ passed the former tolerance of 1% of the largest entry, 3.1e-4 under a tension of 0.03 eV/Å³.
=PointSurface::eval_in_cell= states that the energy and the stress must follow every entry of the cell, and =PointSurface::cauchy_stress= gives the sign, the units and the LAMMPS conversion. =SolidDimerConfig::external_stress= is described as the pressure-signed load it is, not as a Cauchy stress.
=tests/mishin_cu.rs= runs the hop on Mishin copper in a 4×4×4 cell stretched 1.2% and tilted. Diagonals held with the shears free converge in 40 steps at an unchanged volume, shears held in 40, and a pressure-signed surface is refused before the first step. In the same cell without tilt, a box keyed on its extents is refused before the first step. The solid-dimer how-to gives the configuration for a defect saddle at fixed volume.
