=docs/orgmode/howto/pafi.org= is the guide to PAFI for a user with NEB images: what the surface must provide, how =ArcPath= is built from the images and why its hyperplanes follow the spline through them, every =PafiConfig= field with its default, how to choose the arcs, how to read =FreeEnergyProfile= and =HyperplaneAverage= including the block standard error, the =zero_temperature_barrier= check, what the sampler does about thermalization (no equilibration test; check =tau= and =n_eff=), and the comparison with the reference code of Swinburne and Marinica.
The module documentation of =rgsaddle::pafi= has a compiled example on an analytic two-dimensional surface, and the public items of the module are documented. =PafiConfig::max_rounds= is documented as the most passes, the first included, which is what it does.
