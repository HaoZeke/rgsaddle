OH-TST transmission coefficients assign end states by quenching shot endpoints into reference basins, optionally with the intensive permutation-invariant distance of Goswami and Jónsson (=PermutationDistance=), count either Bennett–Chandler or first crossings (=Counting=), and shoot with a mass for every coordinate; the side of the plane is kept as =EndState::Side=. The reversible work runs along any =PlanePath=: PAFI planes along a spline through a minimum energy path, the optimizer's own iterates, straight legs, or a chain of them (=reversible_work_along=). Between neighbouring planes BAR reports the two-state overlap and warns below =ReversibleWorkConfig::min_overlap=, MBAR combines all nodes (=with_mbar=), and =Transport::Gaussian= adds the Gaussian optimal-transport map within each plane, shrunk toward the rigid map by a James–Stein factor so that unresolved changes do not cost overlap. =basin_density= integrates the mean force with a refined not-a-knot spline over a window per side and uses Stein control variates from the Hessian at the minimum. =howto/oh-tst=.
