=PairNetwork= is the kinetic network of a mobile defect next to a fixed one, resolved by symmetry. States are orbits of the integer relative vector and orientation director under a caller-supplied group (=cubic_group= gives O_h), and hop classes are orbits of the unordered configuration pair and saddle director, so one saddle serves a hop and its reverse. =PairMove::orbit= closes a move list under the group. =PairNetwork::sites= asks a closure for each edge's rate, with its hop class, direction and source state, and returns =VolumeSite= rows for =kinetic_recombination_volume= and the hops that leave the radius. Nothing in it names a material.
=matched_capture_radius= closes a site chain at its cutoff against the field-free infinite crystal: an outer hop to =d= is captured with probability =R/d=, =R= matched to the multiplicity-weighted =P r= of the outermost sites. Capture probabilities are affine in =R=, so two solves give it in closed form. =OuterHop= and =MatchedCapture= are its input and output.
