Hyperplane samplers can now consume a generic hard =Region=.  A
=SymmetryUniqueRegion= builds the Voronoi cell of one representative from
caller-supplied point- or space-group operations and reports its explicit
in-plane degeneracy.  Analytic MD reflects at its faces and GHMC rejects
outside proposals without a soft bias.  LAMMPS reflection remains pending
lammpc constrained-step callback support.  =RateMultiplicity= keeps this
degeneracy separate from path or hop multiplicity, preventing symmetry
images from being counted twice.
