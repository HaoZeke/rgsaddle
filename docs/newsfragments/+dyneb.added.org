~DynamicNeb~ (~BandConfig::with_dynamic~, ~BandSession::set_dynamic~) follows ASE 3.29 ~DyNEB~. An interior image whose largest per-atom projected force is strictly below ~fmax~ keeps its last energy and force, stays in the spring and the tangent, and is evaluated again when a neighbour moves the projected force back over the threshold.
~scale_fmax~ scales that threshold by the Euclidean distance from the highest-energy image, which is never given a zero force.
~rgsaddle.ase.DyNEB~ is that band. ~NEB~ still refuses ~dynamic_relaxation~, ~fmax~, and ~scale_fmax~.
C ABI minor 46 adds ~rgsaddle_band_set_dynamic~ and ~rgsaddle_dyneb_mask~. A host checks ~rgsaddle_abi_version()~ for minor 46 or newer.
