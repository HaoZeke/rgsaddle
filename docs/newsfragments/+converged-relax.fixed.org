The Mishin copper pair relaxations stopped after a fixed number of steps and their results were read from wherever the minimizer was. They now relax to a largest atom force, and a run that does not get there is an error.
~pair_field~ ran 30 FIRE steps on the vacancy and the dumbbell; both now reach 1e-4 eV/Å, and L-BFGS gives the same dipole envelopes within 5.04×10⁻⁵ Å (stated tolerance 0.005 Å). The 300, 500 and 700 K envelopes in =benchmarks/defect_pair_run19_field.csv= move from 5.1702–5.3575, 5.0141–5.0749 and 4.7983–4.8651 Å to 5.2465–5.3192, 5.0243–5.0697 and 4.8203–4.8540 Å; the run took 14.8 s before and 33 s after. The run-13 volumes in =benchmarks/defect_pair_run13_volume.csv= move by at most 0.007 Ω and R_eff is unchanged; that run took 95 s before and 120 s after.
The recombination scan ran 60 FIRE steps per placement and read a dumbbell gap outside 1.2–2.5 Å as recombination, giving 54 sites at 300 K and 30 at 400–700 K. Each placement is now followed down the steepest-descent flow by ODE12r to 1e-3 eV/Å and classified by Wigner–Seitz site occupancy, with steepest descent under a 0.02 Å cap as the check; the two agree on all 440 placements within 1.9×10⁻³ Å. Both cells give 46 sites at 300 and 400 K and 42 at 500–700 K, in =benchmarks/defect_pair_shells.csv= and =benchmarks/defect_pair_viv.csv=. The scan took 21.5 min on 8 threads. Converged FIRE and L-BFGS disagree with each other on the 3.63 Å placements perpendicular to the dumbbell on three of the ten lattices, so neither is used to classify.
Shells are enumerated as orbits of the lattice vectors under the dumbbell's symmetry, so a shell's representative no longer depends on floating-point ordering.
The campaign writer wrote the word kappa followed by a trailing space when κ was missing; it now writes =no kappa=, and =benchmarks/defect_pair_finish.csv= and =benchmarks/defect_pair_local.csv= are corrected.
The rate catalogue, the hop searches and the 8788-atom capture campaigns, which relax with =relax_pair_axis= and =pair_recombined=, were not rerun; their recorded values are from the step-capped relaxations.
