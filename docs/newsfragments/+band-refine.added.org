=BandConfig::refine_method= and =BandConfig::refine_threshold= mirror the =opt_method= and =threshold= of eOn's =[Refine]= section, with eOn's defaults: =refine_method= is =None= and =refine_threshold= is 0.5. As in eOn, each step uses the refine stepper when the convergence force is at or under the threshold and the primary stepper otherwise, so a force that rises back over the threshold returns the band to FIRE, and both steppers keep their own state across the switches. The convergence force is the value the =force_tol= stop test reads, and the library converts no units. =BandSession::is_refining= reports which stepper the last step used. It acts on the first-order step of an atomic FIRE band, and the solid-state, quick-min, trust-region, simplified string and growing string bands ignore it. With the default =None= every band steps as before.
